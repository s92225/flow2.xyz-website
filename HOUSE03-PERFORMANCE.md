# House 03 mobile / desktop performance

The entry gate for living/dining is the furnished reception model, structural envelope (walls, floors, soffits and complete stairs), lighting and circulation data. Architectural geometry stays visible as soon as the cover is dismissed, even if other rooms' furniture is blocked. Only separate room furnishings are deferred; paths and direct links request them before crossing into a room. Grounds planting is prepared after the initial indoor frame. Do not swap in screenshots or remove furniture to make entry look ready.

Both mouse buttons use hold-to-look with the mouse direction on desktop; a short left click still selects the floor. Touch drags continue to grab the view. Verify both mouse directions and release with `sa-architecture/tools/showroom/verify-hold-look.mjs`; keyboard arrows do not exercise this path. The two quality controls share one preference; the cover control must work before the first 3D frame. Both device classes adapt to sustained slow frames. Smooth uses a cheaper shadow filter and avoids the extra mirror pass. Desktop Sharp retains live mirrors. Hidden upper furniture must be visible again at the stairs and landings, not just after entering the next room.

Authoring tests: `sa-architecture/tools/showroom/verify-fast-arrival.mjs`, `sa-architecture/verify-house-circulation.mjs`, `sa-architecture/verify-bathroom-privacy.mjs`, and `sa-architecture/verify-working-lift.mjs --touch`. The cold-wall test `sa-architecture/tools/showroom/verify-permanent-envelope.mjs` stalls the deferred rooms to test first-frame walls and stair treads. Both halves of the ground-floor file come from `sa-architecture/tools/showroom/pack-permanent-envelope.mjs`. The paired cold-load benchmark needs BASELINE_URL (unmodified deployed site or local source) and PREVIEW_URL. It applies 5 Mb/s and 85 ms latency in Chromium. It is a lab comparison, not an iPad frame-rate claim.

Asset reductions were generated with `sa-architecture/tools/showroom/pack-fast-arrival.mjs` from the prior reviewed baseline GLBs. Chair and sofa retain their source and fit, and their models have fewer triangles. Three.js r187 is unchanged in version/API: its two existing module files were minified separately with esbuild (`--minify --keep-names --format=esm --legal-comments=inline`), leaving their MIT notices and relative import intact.
